# Source tiers

**Tier 1 (sufficient alone):** AP, Reuters, NYT, WaPo, WSJ, Bloomberg, FT, ProPublica, NPR, PBS, BBC, The Guardian, Politico, Axios, CNN, NBC, ABC, CBS, The Atlantic, The New Yorker, Forbes (news), Fortune, Business Insider (news), Rolling Stone (news desk), Mother Jones, The Hill, Semafor, Punchbowl, Wired, The Verge, 404 Media, The Intercept, Court documents, IG/GAO/OGE/CRS reports, congressional reports.

**Tier 2 (needs a Tier-1 corroboration or primary doc):** Daily Beast, HuffPost, Salon, Raw Story, Vox, Slate, New Republic, Washington Examiner, National Review, Newsweek, Independent, Daily Mail, NY Post.

**Watchdog trackers (use as leads, cite underlying journalism):** CREW, Public Citizen, Campaign Legal Center, Brookings, Just Security, Lawfare, Senate/House committee trackers, American Oversight, POGO.

**Excluded as sole source:** opinion columns, social media posts, partisan blogs, anonymous Substacks.
